## Verified (passes)

- **3.4 pinch**:
  - An open palm and a closed fist never pinch.
  - The OK-sign enters on the 2nd clean frame, and a single lucky frame buys nothing.
  - Hand size and position don't matter: spans 0.08–0.32, anywhere in frame.
  - Works for the mirrored hand, and for a hand tilted 30°.
  - Hysteresis: a half-open hand (gap 0.45) keeps a pinch but can't start one, and opening past 0.55 releases.
  - Probation: folding through into a fist within 400 ms drops the grab, while an established grip may close.
  - An impossible hand (aspect > 6) drops immediately.
  - Fast drag: a 0.62 gap doesn't release a moving hand, but does release a slow one.
  - Holding exempts the signature.
  - The sideways (profile-regime) OK-sign is admitted.
- **3.6 mapping**:
  - Yaw/pitch are 0.012 rad/px; model yaw is free, pitch clamps at ±1.2, pane yaw clamps at ±1.2.
  - Release momentum = x-velocity × 0.012.
  - Stretch is d/d0, centred between the hands, clamped 0.12–12×.
  - R respawns the board.
- **3.5 point**:
  - Nothing is published before the dwell (still 600 ms, then stable 600 ms).
  - A still open hand names the structure under it.
  - A hidden structure in front is skipped.
  - An unlisted mesh names nothing, and the next structure wins.
  - A pinching hand clears the point.
  - A sweeping hand never settles.
  - The second hand points the same way.
