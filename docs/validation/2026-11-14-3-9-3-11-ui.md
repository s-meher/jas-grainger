## 3.11 numbers (synthetic, seeded; NOT a real hand)

30 fps, white 3 px σ jitter at rest, noise-free ramps, 300 px step.

| Filter | rest jitter px σ | lag @ 100 px/s | lag @ 1500 px/s | step to 90 % |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| raw | 3.09 | – | – | – |
| EMA α 0.45 (default, upstream) | 1.71 | 4.1 px | 41 ms | 100 ms |
| One Euro 1 Hz / 0.01 / 1 Hz (`?smooth=oneeuro`) | 1.09 | 4.7 px | 8 ms | 33 ms |

Reacquisition: on a 300 px jump after a dropout, the EMA's first frame lands
135 px along the way and writes it to `cur.history` (fake velocity); the
One Euro path restarts at the hand. Default behaviour is unchanged.

**Testing note: one tracker tab only.** `server.py` hands up to 8 queued
`/cmd` commands to whichever tracker posts `/state` first
(`server.py` `_CMDS[:8]`), so with two tracker tabs open a `bin/board.sh`
command can land in the other tab and appear to do nothing. Pre-existing
server behaviour, found during this session; not introduced by this branch.
Close extra tracker tabs before manual checks and before running the
Python suites.
