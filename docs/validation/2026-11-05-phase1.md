- Means of 0.35–0.69 mm (under one voxel) confirm the DICOM → RAS chain against data this pipeline did not produce.
- Max values on vessel trees include IRCAD's own decimation and branch-end handling.

uniform 1.6 mm step, orientation identity.

- identifying values are **present** in PatientName, PatientBirthDate, PatientSex, InstitutionName, StudyDate, StudyTime and AcquisitionDate
- the verdict is "NOT de-identified"
- no identifiers appear in member names, after a false positive from the generic word "patient" was fixed
- pixel text was not checked
This is why IRCADb outputs stay local.
